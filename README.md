# 🏰 Hogwarts: A Magical Interactive Experience

> *“Hogwarts will always be there to welcome you home.”*

Welcome, young witch or wizard.  
This is not just a website — it is a **living, breathing magical experience**, inspired by the world of *Harry Potter*, crafted through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Step inside Hogwarts.  
Let the castle watch you.  
Let the magic respond.

---

## ✨ About the Project

This project is an **immersive Hogwarts-themed interactive website** designed to recreate the feeling of magic through:
- atmospheric animations
- slow, eerie transitions
- interactive mini-games
- personality-based magical experiences

Rather than retelling the story, this site explores **what it feels like to belong to Hogwarts**.

---

## 🪄 Features

### 🧙 Hogwarts Introduction
- Animated Hogwarts entry screen
- Magical reveal of the castle
- Ambient transitions inspired by wizarding lore

### 🎩 Sorting Hat Experience
- Interactive quiz
- Randomized house selection
- House descriptions with crests and themed styling

### 🍬 Magical Sweets
- **Bertie Bott’s Every Flavour Beans**  
  Hover to discover unpredictable flavours
- **Chocolate Frog Cards**  
  Click to reveal wizard cards  
  ✨ Includes *rare cards* (1% chance)

### 🪞 Room of Reflection
- A slow, poetic self-reflection experience
- Deep, introspective questions
- The room responds to the user’s choices and traits

### 🌲 The Forbidden Forest
- Choice-based survival mini-game
- Multiple paths, dangers, and endings
- Replayable outcomes

### 🛡️ Guardians of Hogwarts
- A lore-driven section explaining how Hogwarts protects itself
- Atmospheric text over a magical background
- Subtle floating magic effects

### 🏠 Hogwarts Houses
- Beautifully styled house cards
- Hover effects and glowing transitions
- House philosophy over surface traits

---

## 🎮 Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — Structure & semantic storytelling  
- **CSS3** — Animations, gradients, magical effects  
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)** — Interactivity, logic, and state  

No frameworks.  
No libraries.  
Pure magic.
